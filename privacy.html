TradeQuest Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

TradeQuest ("the app", "we") is an educational app that teaches investing and trading with lessons, quizzes and simulated trading using virtual money. This policy explains what data we collect, why, and the choices you have. Questions: cristianoronaldo2006legenda@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Account data: your email address (or the private relay address Apple gives us if you use Sign in with Apple), and a user ID. If you sign in with Apple we do not receive your Apple password.
  • Profile: the display name you choose. It is shown to other learners in the community chat.
  • App activity: lesson progress, XP, hearts, streaks, mistakes, practice results, and your simulated portfolio and trades (virtual money only).
  • Community content: messages you post in the community chat, and reports or blocks you make to moderate it.
  • Purchases: if you subscribe, Apple processes the payment. We (through RevenueCat) receive your subscription status and purchase history linked to your user ID. We never see your card or Apple ID payment details.

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, photos, or health data. We do not show ads and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

How we use it

  • To provide the app: sign you in, save your progress across devices, and show your portfolio.
  • To run the community chat safely, including filtering and removing abusive content.
  • To unlock Premium features when you have an active subscription.
  • To fix bugs and keep the service secure.

Who processes your data

  • Supabase hosts our database and sign-in.
  • RevenueCat manages subscription status and receipts.
  • Apple handles Sign in with Apple and in-app payments.
  • Market data providers supply prices; these requests do not include your personal data.

We do not sell your personal data.

Community chat

Messages you post are visible to other users of the app. Do not share personal information in the chat. Other users can report or block you, and we review reports within 24 hours. We may remove content and accounts that break the community rules.

Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account exists. You can delete your account at any time in the app: Profile → Delete Account. This permanently removes your account, progress, portfolio and profile. Deleting your account does not cancel an Apple subscription; cancel it in your Apple ID settings (Settings → your name → Subscriptions). You can also email us to request deletion or a copy of your data.

Children

TradeQuest is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

Educational purpose, not financial advice

TradeQuest is for education only. Nothing in the app is financial, investment or trading advice. All trading in the app is simulated with virtual money.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for significant changes, notify you in the app.
