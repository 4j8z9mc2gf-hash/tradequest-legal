Common questions

How do I restore my Premium purchase?

Open the Premium screen and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID you used to subscribe.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On your iPhone go to Settings → your name → Subscriptions → TradeQuest, or open Profile → Manage subscription in the app. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid being charged for the next period.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Profile → Delete Account. This permanently removes your account and data. It does not cancel a subscription, so cancel it first as described above.

Is the trading real?

No. All trading in TradeQuest is simulated with virtual money. The app is for education and is not financial advice.

How do I report a message or block someone in the community chat?

Tap the menu on any message and choose Report or Block user. We review reports within 24 hours.
